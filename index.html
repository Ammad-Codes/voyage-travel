Sample itinerary

Seven days in Kyoto

A slow week through Japan's old capital. The first five days are below, the last two are left deliberately open.

  2. Day 1

    Arrive in Kyoto

    Private transfer to a machiya townhouse in Higashiyama, a welcome tea and an evening stroll along the Shirakawa canal.

  3. Day 2

    Temples at first light

    Fushimi Inari before the crowds, then a calligraphy lesson with a local master and a quiet kaiseki lunch.

  4. Day 3

    Arashiyama and the bamboo

    Cycle through bamboo groves, cross Togetsukyo Bridge and spend the afternoon at a hidden moss garden.

  5. Day 4

    Hands-on in Nishijin

    Weave silk with a fourth generation artisan, then cook with a family in their home kitchen.

  6. Day 5

    A day in Nara

    Meet the deer in Nara Park, visit Todai-ji and return for a sunset onsen soak.
